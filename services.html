How I Can Help

Focused support for work that needs clarity.

01

Audit and review readiness

I organize artifacts, examine documentation for gaps, and help prepare evidence for a more efficient review.

  • Evidence organization and cleanup
  • Artifact inventories and request tracking
  • Documentation review
  • Audit readiness coordination
02

Governance documentation and controls

I create or refine the documents that define expectations and connect them to the way work is performed.

  • Policies and guidelines
  • Standard operating procedures
  • Control mapping and narratives
  • RMF aligned artifacts
03

Operational assessment

I examine how work moves through an organization, where handoffs fail, and whether responsibility is placed where action can occur.

  • Process and workflow review
  • Gap identification
  • Ownership and handoff clarification
  • Remediation organization
04

Communication

I translate technical findings into language that leaders, clients, and nontechnical teams can use without losing the meaning of the work.

  • Executive ready summaries
  • Stakeholder communications
  • Technical to business translation
  • Awareness and learning materials
