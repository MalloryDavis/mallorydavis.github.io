What Luna Risk Is

Luna Risk is a proof-of-concept for GRC learning content: a way to make risk, control behavior, evidence habits, and cyber decision-making easier to recognize and discuss.

The work connects security awareness with governance reality. It keeps the language accessible without softening the risk.
