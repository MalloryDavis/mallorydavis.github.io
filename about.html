The GRC Layer

I had been building control logic before I had the technical language for it.

In operations, messy onboarding exposed the same issues GRC cares about: incomplete intake, inconsistent handoffs, unclear ownership, and shifting standards.

The cost showed up in delays, refunds, lost sales, fewer renewals, and clients who felt misunderstood.

GRC gave that pattern a sharper frame: define the standard, preserve the record, assign ownership, and support the next decision.
