About

Technology risk work depends on more than technical answers.

I help technical teams explain the risk clearly enough for leaders and clients to act.

An operations lens for Technology Risk and GRC.

My professional foundation was built in environments where process gaps affected people, performance, and trust.

Complex operations taught me to recognize weak handoffs, missing records, uncertain ownership, and standards that changed depending on who was involved.

I bring that judgment into control documentation, assessment support, risk analysis, and remediation work. The goal is to understand what is happening, show why it matters, and help the right people decide what comes next.

I am especially effective when technical findings need to be understood by a nontechnical audience without losing the meaning of the risk.
